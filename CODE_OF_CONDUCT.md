# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be civil and argue about the work, not the person making it.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space this project uses.

## Expected

- Assume the person you are replying to is competent and acting in good faith.
- Criticize claims, code, and mathematics as sharply as you like. Rigor is the point of
  this project and a wrong proof should be called wrong.
- Say what would change your mind.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about a person or a group.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information.

## A note specific to this project

AutoTao produces mathematical claims automatically, and most of them are wrong — that is
what `attempts/LOG.md` is for. Pointing out that a claimed result does not hold is a
contribution, not an attack, and the correct response to it is a corrected ledger line.

Conversely: presenting unverified machine output as a result, here or elsewhere, with this
project's name attached, is the one thing that would genuinely damage it. Do not.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to the maintainer through a private GitHub security advisory or
direct message. Reports stay confidential. The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete
comments and may block accounts.
